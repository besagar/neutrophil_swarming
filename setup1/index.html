Setup 1 — Polarization in a uniform cue

A single neutrophil sits in a spatially uniform background concentration \(L\) of chemoattractant. Because \(\nabla L = 0\) there is no chemotactic drive; the polarization vector \(\mathbf{P}\) performs Brownian dynamics in a rotationally symmetric Ginzburg–Landau well. In nondimensional form,

\[ \frac{dP_\alpha}{d\tilde t} = (\mathcal{L} - 1)\, P_\alpha + \lambda \big(|\mathbf{P}|^2 - |\mathbf{P}|^4\big)\, P_\alpha + \sqrt{2\, \vartheta\, \mathcal{L}}\; \eta_\alpha(\tilde t), \quad \langle \eta_\alpha(\tilde t)\, \eta_\beta(\tilde t')\rangle = \delta_{\alpha\beta}\, \delta(\tilde t - \tilde t'). \]

Equivalently, overdamped gradient flow \(dP_\alpha/d\tilde t = -\partial \tilde F / \partial P_\alpha + \sqrt{2 \vartheta \mathcal{L}}\, \eta_\alpha\) for the free energy

\[ \tilde F(\mathbf{P}; \mathcal{L}, \lambda) = -\tfrac{1}{2}\,(\mathcal{L} - 1)\,|\mathbf{P}|^2 - \tfrac{1}{4}\,\lambda\,|\mathbf{P}|^4 + \tfrac{1}{6}\,\lambda\,|\mathbf{P}|^6. \]

The three independent dimensionless knobs are:

\(\mathcal{L} = L / L_c\) cue level (only knob that carries the physical \(L\)) \(\lambda = u^2 / (w\, r_0\, L_c)\) intrinsic — strength of the cubic + quintic terms \(\vartheta = \theta\, w / (u\, r_0)\) intrinsic noise level

The bifurcation depends on the single combination \((\mathcal{L} - 1)/\lambda\). \(\mathcal{L} > 1\) gives a polarized phase with \(|\mathbf{P}|^2 = \tfrac12\big(1 + \sqrt{1 + 4(\mathcal{L}-1)/\lambda}\big)\); for \(1 - \lambda/4 < \mathcal{L} < 1\) this minimum coexists with a metastable \(P = 0\); below \(\mathcal{L} = 1 - \lambda/4\) only \(P = 0\) survives.

Original dimensional equation

From the source spec \(\,\)ginzburg_landay_neutrophils.md:

\[ \frac{dp}{dt} = r_0(L - L_c)\, p + u\, p^3 - w\, p^5 + \sqrt{2 \theta L}\, \xi(t), \quad \langle \xi(t)\, \xi(t')\rangle = \delta(t - t'). \]

The nondim form above follows from

\[ \mathcal{L} = L/L_c, \quad P = p/p_0, \quad \tilde t = t/t_0, \quad t_0 = 1/(r_0 L_c), \quad p_0 = \sqrt{u/w}, \] \[ \lambda = \frac{u^2}{w\, r_0\, L_c}, \quad \vartheta = \frac{\theta\, w}{u\, r_0}. \]
Boltzmann steady state (the green dashed curves)

Because the dynamics is overdamped gradient flow with additive isotropic noise of strength \(D_{\rm eff} = \vartheta\, \mathcal{L}\), the stationary Fokker–Planck equation admits the closed-form Gibbs measure

\[ P_{\rm ss}(\mathbf{P}) \;\propto\; \exp\!\left(-\frac{\tilde F(\mathbf{P})}{\vartheta\, \mathcal{L}}\right). \]

For the histogram of \(|\mathbf{P}|\):

\[ P_{\rm rad}(r) \;\propto\; r^{\,d-1}\, \exp\!\big(-\tilde F(r) / (\vartheta\, \mathcal{L})\big), \quad d = 1\ \text{or}\ 2. \]

The 2D Jacobian \(r^{d-1}\) shifts the radial peak away from the origin even when \(\tilde F\) has its minimum at \(r = 0\). The mean magnitude \(\langle |\mathbf{P}| \rangle (\mathcal{L})\) shown below is the corresponding numerical integral; in the broken-symmetry phase it is the right diagnostic since \(\langle \mathbf{P} \rangle = 0\) by rotational (or reflection) invariance of \(\tilde F\).
